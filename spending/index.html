    Categorisation rules

    Categories

    "Excluded" categories (like transfers between your own accounts or YouTrip top-ups) are left out of spending and income so nothing is counted twice.

    Exchange rates

    YouTrip wallets hold foreign currency. Totals are shown in SGD using these rates (1 unit = S$). Defaults are approximate; set them to what you actually paid.

    Getting your statements

    • DBS/POSB account (CSV): digibank online → My Accounts → pick the account → Transaction History → choose the period → Download.
    • DBS card (CSV): digibank online → Cards → pick the card → Statements / Transaction History → Download.
    • DBS e-Statement (PDF): digibank → e-Statements → download the PDF. PDF reading is best-effort; check the preview before importing.
    • YouTrip: YouTrip app → Me → Statement → pick months → export (CSV if offered, otherwise PDF), then send it to this device.
